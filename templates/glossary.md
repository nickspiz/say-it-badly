## Number habits

- "oh" in a version is `0`.
- "dot", "point" both mean `.` in versions and domains.
- Spoken amounts are never final. Verify before paying or billing.

## Negation words to watch

"don't", "do not", "never", "without", "except", "skip". If one may be missing or extra and the action is not trivially reversible, ask.
